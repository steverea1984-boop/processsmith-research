## Library boundary

This public repository is authoritative for ProcessSmith business research. General learning and personal technology research remain in `F:\03_Learning\Research`. Cross-library analysis may read both catalogs, but it must not copy report bodies or private metadata into this repository.

The research-method and monthly-watch documents under `research-architecture/` govern how research is produced. They are library policy, not standalone research projects, so they are linked from the root README rather than assigned project IDs.
